Rio Grande do Sul, Brazil

The Rio Grande do Sul state has notority for being a state of culuture and traditions. Over the years the culture from Europe, such as German and Italy, mixed with some traditions from Uruguay and Argentina to create what people call today the Gaucho culture. Our traditions include a unique way of making barbecue, which people around Brazil state that is the best barbecue they ever had, dancing steps and a tea known as chimarrão.
